# Project verification

- Firearms are daily equipment, not automatic shelter surplus: keep serviceable carried rifles (including temporarily dry ones) and their matching ammo while they fit. Capacity trimming preserves a usable primary gun and its calibre before a knife or a second gun, and splits stacked duplicate guns instead of storing both. A useful stored gun can replace lesser carried items through an atomic inventory/stash exchange; do not lose displaced items or seed ammunition during inspection. Secondary guns that cannot fit must not keep sending an armed bot home. Broken guns can be replaced, and a removed gun's spare calibre is stored in the same transaction. `BotGunAmmoRegression` covers these transfers, real limits, duplicates, broken guns, repeated visits and unchanged ammunition; retain the existing stash/save checks in `SurvivorBotsAndCombatRegression`.

Discovery keeps its destination when an old site's veto expires, completes on actual arrival, then enables a normal raid at the newly observed base. - Bot survival scoring: `bot_score_value` (shared by the monitor, the advisor prompt and regressions) makes surviving the supreme objective — +2 pts per minute alive, +10 per known location, +50/+75 per bot/player kill and **−200 per death** (a death erases 100 minutes of progress, so an average life cannot pay for itself). `BOT_STAKE_FULL_SEC` (2700 s) matures `_bot_stake` 0→1 over the current life: high-stake bots sense predators +6 m farther (`_bot_predator_sense_radius`), retreat at up to +12 hp (`_bot_retreat_hp`), flee nemeses at lower fear, retreat sooner when outgunned and discount remembered-risk destinations deeper (`_ai_poi_memory_factor`). Stake is zero for fresh spawns and the dead, so combat regressions that spawn new bots are unaffected; tests override `_bot_stake` in the subclass. The advisor system prompt states the survival economy and receives `alive_min`/`stake`/`score` per bot. `BotScoreStakeRegression` covers the score formula, stake maturation (via the pure `bot_stake_from_life`), and every integration point. Never reset `mem.deaths` for scoring — it also drives nemesis, adaptations and the death curriculum. In the expected-utility engine the stake raises `risk_aversion` ONLY in proportion to threat evidence (`threat_p` posterior: enemy sighting or remembered zone combat) — in a calm zone a mature bot explores exactly like a fresh one, so caution never becomes paralysis. Anti-paralysis: `_roam_still_sec` accumulates sedentary time within `ROAM_RADIUS` (35 m, re-anchored by any excursion) and `_bot_restlessness` ramps in after `ROAM_GRACE_SEC`, boosting explore/scavenge/forage benefits and deflating rest/fortify in the next plan decision (never an interruption); night, pre-night, sensed threats, hp <50 or critical hunger/thirst suppress it — survival stays supreme.
