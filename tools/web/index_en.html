The story

The morning the world ended there were no flames and no heroes. Only silence.

The cars just stopped on the roads. The lights went out one by one. And the houses — with their pantries, their coats and the rifles forgotten in the attics — waited for owners who never came back.

You arrived later. With nothing but the clothes on your back.

· · ·

In Last Day there are no miracles: what you find is all there is. The world is a finite inventory shared by everyone living in it. Every opened can, every looted backpack, every spent match is gone forever — for you and for everyone else. Just like real life: when something runs out, it runs out.

The first days are for gathering: fallen sticks, river stones, rags from an old shirt. With bare hands you will craft the essentials — a sharpened stick, a stone knife, a tendon rope. Then comes the hunt: a bow strung with improvised fiber, a spear thrown at the last second, the campfire that turns raw meat into one more night of life. And when the village shelves are bare forever, the world enters its second phase: the age of those who know how to fish, sow, heal and endure.

· · ·

Other survivors walk this same world. They are your greatest luck or your last mistake: every resource they consume is one you will never see again, and every corpse is a stash someone left behind. At night the wolves hunt in packs; the cold shows no mercy to wet clothes, and your sight dims as the blood runs out.

There is no rescue. There is no written ending.
Only each day's dawn and the same question:
How many more days can you survive on what is left?

Features

  • Co-op multiplayer Up to 4 players per session on the official dedicated server, with a live player list.
  • Realistic survival Hunger, thirst, cold, energy, body temperature, foot wounds, wet clothes that chill you.
  • Abandoned town & village Houses, a four-story skyscraper and the clock tower square, all lootable: food, clothes, tools, weapons…
  • Finite loot The loot is what there is — nothing new respawns. You must survive on what you find.
  • Day/night cycle & weather Sunsets, fog, rain that soaks your clothes and nights that kill with cold.
  • Building Campfires for cooking and warmth, persistent shelters to sleep in and store supplies.
  • AI wildlife Hunting wolf packs, deer, foxes, bears, birds and fish. Wolves can be tamed with food.
  • Combat & stealth Fists, blades, a rifle with held breath; crouch and use the night to your advantage.
  • Fishing & boat Fish in the river and the lake, and travel the water in a rowboat.
  • Death with consequences When you die, your corpse keeps all your loot: you can come back for it… if you get there before the others.
  • Your character stays When you log out, your character remains in the world: wolves and other players can attack it if they find it. Sheltering or hiding well is the only way to come back alive.
  • Dedicated server Persistent world during the session — every player connects to the official server.

How to play

  1. Explore the village. You spawn with just your clothes. Enter the houses and gather food, clothes and tools — loot does not regenerate and the others want it too.
  2. Cover the essentials. Eat, drink from the river, find warm clothes and something to defend yourself with. A campfire saves your first night.
  3. Build your base. Raise a shelter, store supplies and sleep to recover. The backpack increases what you can carry.
  4. Manage your energy. Sprinting burns stamina — without it you can only walk. Cold and rain drain your body; wet clothes are your enemy.
  5. Survive the night. Wolves come out to hunt, the temperature drops and the darkness is real. Torch, fire and shelter make the difference.
  6. If you die, you start over. New character, empty inventory — but your corpse stays there with all your gear, waiting for you to recover it.

Your tamed wolf

Wild wolves hunt in packs and are lethal at night — but with enough food you can earn one as a permanent companion.

How to tame it

  1. Get meat. Hunt deer or foxes: the wolf only accepts raw meat or game scraps on the ground.
  2. Stay close while it eats. Drop the meat within its reach and stay within 15 m — if you move away, the meal does not count.
  3. Three meals and it is yours. After the third valid meal the wolf will be tamed. Carrying meat in your hand also calms it: it prowls at a distance instead of attacking.

Commands

F aiming at itToggles Follow me / Stay
XWhistle: 1× comes to your side and follows without attacking · 2× attacks whatever is nearby
OCommand menu: Follow · Stay (guard area) · Patrol · Stop attacking · Attack a nearby target — with its live stats: health and food bars, current order, activity and distance

What it does for you

  • It defends you. It bites whoever hurts you — players, wild wolves, bears — and counterattacks whatever you attack.
  • It hunts with you. Chases deer and foxes on command or instinct; after eating it leaves meat scraps you can skin.
  • It warns you of hunger. It whines when it needs food — leave meat on the ground to feed it.
  • It guards your body on logout. If you disconnect, it stays next to your character and barely uses food (it can last almost a full day without eating).
  • It survives restarts. The bond is saved with the game: it is still yours after reconnecting or after a server restart.
  • One per player. You can only have one loyal wolf at a time — every player in the session can have their own.
  • Mourning. If you die, it stands guard by your corpse for a minute before turning wild forever.

The survivors

Four survivors with their own personality live in the same world as you — they loot houses, hunt, fish, plant gardens, sleep and shelter from the cold. They remember what you did to them: they can become allies… or your worst encounter.

  • Elena — the ally Curious and sociable: the first to approach, share and team up. Not aggressive but with a huge survival instinct — she minds her food, her fire and her shelter. With her knife she is only dangerous when cornered.
  • Carlos — the steady hand Brave and cooperative: fights with his axe without hesitation, then comes back to lend the group a hand. Equal parts muscle and head — a good ally, a bad idea to turn against you.
  • Kael — the lone wolf Military profile: aggressive, distrustful and armed with an assault rifle. He does not cooperate or team up — he watches every corner. Approach very slowly or do not approach at all: he is the most dangerous encounter on the map.
  • Ataulfo — the explorer Curiosity made flesh: he roams the entire map end to end, always on his own. He defends himself with a knife but would rather vanish than fight. If you see tracks in the middle of nowhere, they are probably his.
